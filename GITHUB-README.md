# GitHub, in plain words

**GitHub** is a free website that keeps your files safe online and remembers every version of them. It's where your MAIURTH and Animation Corner pages live right now:

- https://willdutcher.github.io/dad_test_page/
- https://willdutcher.github.io/dad_test_page/maiurth.html

## Why it's worth having

- **Free web pages.** GitHub will put your pages on the internet at no cost. You get a link you can text or email to anyone.
- **A backup that doesn't forget.** If your computer dies, your work is still there.
- **Every version, saved.** Changed a scene and liked the old one better? You can go back to any earlier version.
- **Easy sharing.** Friends, family or anyone you're working with can see the latest version without you emailing files around.

## You don't have to learn it

GitHub has a reputation for being technical, and it can be. But you don't need to touch any of that. Claude can do all of it for you: set up your account's pages, upload your work, update it, and undo changes.

All you need to know is how to ask. For example:

- "Put my new MAIURTH scene online and give me the link."
- "Update the intro page with these changes."
- "Make me a new page for my next character and put it on GitHub."
- "Go back to last week's version of the story."
- "Make a backup of all my MAIURTH files."

The one thing you'll do yourself is make a free account at https://github.com/signup, then connect it to Claude when it asks. After that, just ask.
